Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

NightGuard does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your information stays on your device.

Information We Collect

NightGuard does not collect any personal information. We do not have servers, databases, or any infrastructure that could store your data.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using:

  • SwiftData - Sleep records, schedules, and partner information
  • UserDefaults (App Group) - Shared settings between the app and extensions
  • iCloud Keychain - Subscription status (managed by Apple)

Screen Time API Data

NightGuard uses Apple's Screen Time API (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity) to block apps during your scheduled bedtime. This data:

  • Is processed entirely on your device
  • Is never transmitted to our servers (we don't have any)
  • Is subject to Apple's privacy policies

Notifications

NightGuard may send local notifications for:

  • Bedtime reminders
  • Wake-up notifications
  • Bypass attempt alerts

These are local notifications only. No data is sent to external servers.

Third-Party Services

NightGuard uses the following Apple services:

  • StoreKit 2 - For in-app purchases and subscriptions. Payment processing is handled by Apple.
  • FamilyControls - For app blocking functionality

Children's Privacy

NightGuard is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
